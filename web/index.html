MeshCore Web

Chat on MeshCore channels through a pyMeshRoom radio. Your private key stays in this browser: every message is encrypted and signed here.

Anyone can log in and read. You can send once this radio has heard your node on the mesh: the public key that goes with your private key, in an advert from your MeshCore app (a flood advert reaches it from further away).
